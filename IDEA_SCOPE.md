IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint

The idea, in one line: Alerts a Shopify Plus team on Slack the moment something in checkout starts losing them money.

v1 (this sprint): one alert only: orders suddenly stop vs the store's normal pattern. Next alerts added one at a time after it works on a real store.

Why me: I have a very deep sense of how checkout works, how to extract maximum revenue, and what Shopify Plus merchants actually need from their checkout.

GOAL
The one goal they hire it for: Money
Delta 4: Today most merchants don't know when something is wrong with checkout. To find out, they hire CRO people who analyse heatmaps and weeks of data, charge a lot, and answer weeks later. With my product, checkout is watched 24x7 and the team is alerted within minutes.
The sin it rides: FOMO

USER
Who it is for: Shopify Plus stores outside India with 3k+ orders a month.
The trigger: A developer pushes a theme change, or marketing sends a campaign, and checkout quietly breaks.
Today's path: Nobody is watching checkout. One store owner was on holiday and missed the email, and the dev team didn't notice. Customers could not add to cart for 5 days. Found only when sales looked wrong. About $100k lost.
Who they trust on this decision: Their agency, their CRO team, or their ecommerce manager.
Would they pay?: Checkout Pulse by PDQ charges per order. I would start at $0.10 per order, so about $300/month for a 3k-orders store.

PRODUCT
Onboarding: Install the app, connect a Slack channel, and a test alert lands within 5 minutes.
The core loop: The app watches orders per hour against the store's normal pattern. When orders drop far below normal, it posts an alert to the store's Slack channel.
Coming back: It is like insurance. You may not get an alert for weeks, but the one you get can save a lot of money.
The AI-first part: AI learns each store's normal pattern (hour, weekday, campaigns) so it only alerts on a real drop, not a quiet hour.

MARKET
Tailwinds: Merchants prepare checkouts for BFCM in Oct–Nov, so selling week lands while they are most worried about checkout breaking. For high-volume Plus stores any day can be a BFCM: one failed code (HELLO20 failing in 2,000 sessions) can leak around $15,000.
Competitors: Checkout Pulse by PDQ, CheckoutGuard, Pretty Damn Quick
Size and fit: 1 merchant I know personally (lost about $100k to this). 4 contacted, 0 replied so far.

v2 (not this sprint): the other alert types, CRO intel, industry benchmarks, shipping insights, predictions.
